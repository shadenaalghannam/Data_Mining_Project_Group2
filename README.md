# IT326-Data-Mining-Project_Group2
Description
This project aims to determine whether water is safe for human consumption. This can be challenging because water quality depends on several physical and chemical characteristics.

# **Objective**
The main objective is to classify water samples into two categories: potable and non-potable, based on different water quality measurements. In addition, clustering techniques will be used to identify groups of water samples with similar water quality characteristics.

# **Motivation**
We chose this project because water quality is important for human health and daily life. Identifying whether water is safe for drinking can help reduce potential health risks. The dataset contains various water quality measurements that can help us understand the factors related to water potability and apply classification and clustering techniques to the data.
